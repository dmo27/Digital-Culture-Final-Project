Social Media Migration

Social Media migration might be defined as a change in the social media used by an age group. Many respondents only mentioned when they started to use their first social media. However, some answers, mostly from respondents aged 40 to 50, allow us to understand and identify some migrations. However, migration shouldn't be understood as leaving a place for another since this migration is, most of the time, more a combination than a replacement.

We can see that many of the 40-to-50-year-olds "migrated" to new social media and stopped using exclusively Facebook, one of their favourite social media as you can see in the analysis of the practises. Many answers show that this migration started in 2019 and accelerated in the last two years. "I use Instagram since 2019, I started using Facebook earlier," "Snapchat and Instagram for two years." "Facebook since 2009, Instagram since a month." "Facebook since 2006, the other (Insta and Snap) later."

One of our theories is that the older "migrate" to the media used by the younger, many of them joined Facebook in the 2000s when it was created and was seen as something for young people. Now, as it is seen as outdated by many young people, they keep using it but also download new apps, especially Instagram and, to a lesser extent, Snapchat and TikTok, to see what the younger generation do. One of the respondent's answer is along those lines as he or she wrote that he or she started to use Instagram and Snapchat to see what their children post and do.
